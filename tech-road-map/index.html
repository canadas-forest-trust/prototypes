Tech Road Map

Notes behind the investor-deck Tech Road Map slide. What’s built, what’s next, and what the raise is for.

Where we started What we’ve developed What this raise unlocks Future state

Where we started

A single Hub page with forest stats. Onboarding was manual, ops ran through Zoho, contracts lived in Google Docs.

Single-page Smart Forest™ Hub with basic forest data

Partners could see trees and a forest story. Staff still did most of the work around it by hand.

Manual onboarding, Zoho-backed ops, Google Doc contracts

Every new partner meant time in Zoho, email, and docs.

What we’ve developed

One partner path from lead to Hub to campaign, and one staff platform instead of Zoho CRM. Built and running on preview. Production is still the legacy stack until we cut over.

Branded partner Hub: EN/FR, Learning Centre, marketing assets, multi-forest, LGG badges

Logo and colour, English and French, Learning Centre, marketing assets, multiple forests, LGG badges when the program uses them.

Retail stack: Shopify app, contribution API, live tree counters, RESTOR planting data

Merchants add trees at checkout. The API attributes them, live counters show them on the partner’s site, planting is tied to RESTOR. Shopify 2.0 is still ahead of us.

Passwordless login and self-serve partner onboarding

Staff send an invite. The partner signs in with a one-time code, sets branding, and lands in their Hub.

Public campaign pages with checkout (partner fundraising)

Partners can publish a campaign page and take contributions. Checkout works on preview (Stripe test). Live payments and payouts are still to do.

In-house staff platform: CRM, tasks, outreach, programs, agreements + e-sign

Pipeline, tasks, outreach, programs, and in-house agreement signing in one admin. This is how we replace Zoho CRM.

One Postgres-based platform for Hub, billing, and CRM — ready to go live

Hub, contributions, billing, and CRM on one Postgres database. No Zoho in the new code. Built on preview — not on production DNS yet.

What this raise unlocks

Take the rebuilt platform to production, then use Hub for revenue — campaigns, retail, certificates — without adding ops people in lockstep with partners.

Take it live Cut over to the new stack and get off Zoho.
Sell through Hub Campaigns, retail, certificates — partner audiences become attributed trees.
Scale the team’s output Onboarding, nurture, and fulfillment in the product so growth isn’t more CX headcount.

Take the rebuilt platform live at scale and fully exit Zoho

Ship the new stack to production. One system for staff and partners. Off Zoho for ops data and for CRM.

Shopify 2.0 plus apps for other major retail platforms

Current Shopify app is live. 2.0 is a better merchant experience and a path onto other platforms without a custom build each time.

Scale partner campaigns — live payments, payouts, more partners

Off test checkout. Live payments, partner payouts, and the ops to run more than a handful of campaigns.

Certificates, sales reports, and automated tree fulfillment

Monthly loop: partners report sales, trees get fulfilled, certificates go out — without the spreadsheet pile.

Forest Impact Reporting and Hub analytics for partner boards

Partners already ask for numbers they can take to their board. Self-serve reporting is how we stop answering that by email.

CRM × Hub × email automations that grow CLV without linear headcount

Onboard, nurture, and renew in the product we already have — not another tool to log into.

Future state

After cutover: signed deals write into the account, partners edit their own assets, and education can certify.

Provision accounts from signed agreements

E-sign is built. Next is Apply: a signed agreement writes terms, dates, and Hub defaults so CX isn’t re-keying the deal.

Partner-editable marketing assets

The library is there. An editor in Hub means partners make on-brand assets without a design ticket.

Certification-based education modules

Learning Centre is live. Certification modules would let partners assign a program, not just browse content.
