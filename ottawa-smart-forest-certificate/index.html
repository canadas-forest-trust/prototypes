Smart Forest certificate
Design directions
Five design directions for the Smart Forest tree certificate. Each has a main layout and a variation, and is shown across all five occasions. We need product to pick one direction and one layout to take into production.
Format
US Letter, landscape
Output
Print + PDF
Occasions
Dedication, Memorial, New child, Birthday, Service award
Options
5 directions × 2 layouts
01 — How to review
Compare the two large Dedication certificates in each direction first. The small sheets beneath show how that direction adapts to the other four occasions. Every sheet has an ID, such as 4a, that you can quote in feedback.
02 — Fixed in every direction
The CFTC logo; the partner logos (City of Ottawa, Cool Climate Club); the QR code; one Smart Forest name per partner account; no tree species and no planting site. The same data fields fill every layout.
03 — What we need back
One direction, one layout (main or variation), and any change requests. Quote a sheet ID, such as 4a.
